## 8. Methodology and assumptions

### Data sources

- **Flight discovery:** `/api/flights/current?airport=<ICAO>&landed_hours=24&range_nm=25` against all 7 Front Range fields (KBDU, KBJC, KAPA, KFNL, KEIK, KLMO, KGXY). Raw counts: KBDU=296, KBJC=239, KAPA=63, KFNL=55, KEIK=331, KLMO=335, KGXY=25. After union by (tail, takeoff_ts): 604 unique pre-window flights; 201 retained after windowing and 5 nm radius filter; **162 retained** after a final 5-minute bucket dedupe (same physical cycle surfaced by multiple airport feeds collapses to one row, keeping the loudest closest-approach).
- **Per-flight tracks:** `/api/adsb/track/{icao}?since=<takeoff_ts>` — pulled for 237 flights with resolvable ICAO mappings (via the live snapshot + the curated KBDU fleet table). 4 returned 404 (track not in today's live cache); 59 candidates had no ICAO mapping from the live snapshot and fell back to the `worst_segment.points` array embedded in the `/api/flights/current` response (the loudest sampled segment of the path).
- **Window:** flights whose takeoff or landing fell inside `2026-05-30T00:00:00Z..2026-05-31T23:59:59Z`, or that straddled the window start.

### dBA estimation

Implemented exactly per the spec formula (see `noise/Location_noise report.md`):

```
agl   = max(alt_ft_msl - 5288, 100)
base  = TYPE_BASE_DBA[type] or 72 (default trainer baseline)
vert  = 6 * log2(agl/1000)  if agl > 1000 else 0
slant = hypot(dist_ft, agl)
lat   = 3 * log2(slant/500) if slant > 500 else 0
dBA   = max(0, base - vert - lat)
```

Engineless types (PW6, ASK21, K21, DISC, DG10/15/80/1T, NIMB, VENT, JS1J, ASTR, LS4, GLID, AS21, SZD, PIK, STD, CLUB, LAK) return 0 — the glider produces no engine noise. On an aerotow the tow plane (PA25/PA18/HUSK) is the noise source, scored separately.

### AGL calc

AGL is `alt_msl - 5288` (KBDU field elevation), floored at 100 ft. The listener's terrain elevation in Frasier Meadows is ~5,300 ft, very close to KBDU field elevation, so using field elevation as the listener floor is accurate to within ~50 ft.

### Closest-approach geometry

For each flight we walk every track point, compute equirectangular slant range to the listener, and retain the (lat, lon, alt, ts) at the minimum. Distance formula: `dlat * 364567 ft/deg`, `dlon * 364567 * cos(mlat)` — accurate to <1% at GA spatial scales.

### Data gaps and caveats

- 59 of 300 candidates (~20%) lacked a live-snapshot ICAO mapping (their squawk had aged out). For those flights we fell back to the `worst_segment.points` (a sparse 5-7-point sample of their highest-impact path segment) rather than a dense track. Closest-approach geometry on those is approximate.
- 4 ICAOs returned 404 from the track endpoint (typical when the ICAO has dropped out of today's live cache).
- Phase fields reflect each aircraft's snapshot state, not the phase *at the moment of closest approach*. A flight currently on the ground at KEIK whose track passed within 2 nm of the listener during airwork still appears with phase=`on_ground` — that's the snapshot, not the audible event.
- The `est_dba` formula assumes free-field propagation. Real-world dBA at a residential receiver will be 3-8 dB lower due to ground absorption, tree cover, and house attenuation. Treat reported numbers as upper bounds for outdoor reception at the listener point.
- For aircraft without an entry in `TYPE_BASE_DBA`, the default base of 72 dBA was used. Affected types are noted in the per-type table where mean ≈ 72 with low spread.
- The 'noise-abatement-zone overlap' table is heuristic (radial 2 nm + altitude < 7500 MSL). A polygon-precise test would use `/api/noise-zones?airport=KBDU` and a point-in-polygon check against each track point.
